2. Physical construction

The vessel is a stainless steel body with a polypropylene handle and lid.
A removable mesh filter sits in the spout. Colour: brushed steel / black handle.

Fill between the min mark and the max mark. The usable capacity is 1.5 L
when filled to the max line. Do not fill above the max mark.

Intended use: Boiling water for domestic use. Not for commercial catering
or outdoor cooking.

The heating element is concealed in the base of the jug. Do not operate
the kettle on an induction hob. Keep the rating label on the base visible.

3. Packing, care, and end of life

Goods in the carton: kettle body, 360-degree power base, unbranded leaflet.

Automatic shut-off: a steam sensor switches the kettle off at boil.
Boil-dry protection cuts power if the vessel is empty.

Cleaning: descale with a 1:1 white vinegar solution. Do not immerse the
power base. Do not use abrasive pads on the heating plate.

End of life: this product must not be disposed of with household waste;
take it to a WEEE collection point.

Factory warranty: 12 months against manufacturing defects. Retail warranty
is the buyer's responsibility. Batch coding: YYWW plus line id on the base.
