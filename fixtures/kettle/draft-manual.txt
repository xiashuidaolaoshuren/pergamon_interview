User instructions

Acme Rapid Kettle
Model ARK-1500
Manufacturer: Acme Appliances Ltd.

Please read these instructions before first use and keep them for later
reference. Retain the carton until you have checked that nothing is missing.

Technical data
Rated voltage: 230 V
Rated frequency: 50 Hz
Protection class: I

Place the kettle on a stable, heat-resistant surface away from the edge
of the worktop. Do not use it outdoors.

--- page 2 ---
About this kettle

This cordless kettle is for household hot-water preparation. Place it on
a dry surface and keep the cord clear of the hob.

The jug holds 1.7 L to the maximum mark. A water window on the handle
shows the level. The lid opens with the button on the handle.

If the kettle was transported on its side, let it stand upright for
30 minutes before connecting the base.

Keep children away from the steam when the kettle switches off.

--- page 3 ---
Before first use, fill to max, boil, and discard the water twice.

To boil
1. Sit the 360-degree power base on a level surface and plug it in.
2. Fill the kettle, close the lid, and place it on the base.
3. Press the switch down. The indicator lamp lights.
4. The kettle switches off automatically when the water boils. Lift the jug to pour.

The kettle includes: kettle, power base, and spout filter.

If you lift the kettle off the base, it switches off. Replace it and
press the switch again to restart.

--- page 4 ---
Safety and care

A boil-dry cut-out operates if the kettle is switched on with little or
no water. Allow it to cool, refill, then restart.

Descaling: use a kettle descaler or equal parts water and white vinegar.
Rinse thoroughly. Do not put the kettle or the base in a dishwasher.
Do not immerse the base in water.

When the product is worn out, do not put it in household rubbish.
Deliver it to a local electrical-waste (WEEE) collection site.

Keep the kettle out of reach of children. Do not open the lid while boiling.
